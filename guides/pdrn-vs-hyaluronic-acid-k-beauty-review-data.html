The last two rows come from capped samples of up to 500 helpful review records. They are metadata-flag shares inside those samples, not buyer-wide repurchase or long-term-use rates.

Does this show that PDRN is better?

No. These are three different finished products, not a controlled comparison of isolated ingredients. Formula, concentration, texture, audience, price, promotion, and review timing can all differ. The product-level signals can help narrow a shopping list, but they cannot establish that PDRN caused Anua's larger repeat-purchase flag share.

Which comparison is useful?

  • Broader dry-skin context: Anua, because 52% selected dry in the captured skin-type poll.
  • Broader oily-skin context: Wellage and Torriden, at 14% and 13% respectively, versus Anua at 4%.
  • Hydration-led poll context: all three are strongly represented; Wellage leads by 4–6 percentage points.
  • Confidence from review volume: Anua has the broadest captured rating base, while Torriden's 4.9 rating comes from the smallest base of the three.
