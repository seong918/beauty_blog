How to read the ranking

The concern-poll column answers a narrow question: among the displayed poll responses for that product, what share selected the soothing-redness label? It does not measure redness reduction. It also does not mean that 82% of all Bring Green reviewers had redness or experienced a result.

Large percentage, small base

Physiogel shows why percentage and sample context belong together. Its soothing-redness share is second highest, but its captured rating base of 86 is dramatically smaller than the bases for Bring Green, Mediheal, and Cicaplast. That does not invalidate the signal; it makes it less settled.

Match the comparison base to your question

  • More oily-skin representation: Bring Green at 31%, followed by Celladix at 21%.
  • More dry-skin representation: Cicaplast Baume B5 at 50%, followed by Physiogel at 45%.
  • Largest aggregate context: Mediheal at 41,856 captured reviews.

These lanes help you choose which full analyses to inspect. They do not replace checking the current label, individual sensitivities, or professional advice when redness is persistent or medically concerning.

Method and limits

All numbers are dated aggregate displays captured from a Korean K-beauty retail platform. No individual review text, name, or photo is reproduced. Read the developer story, methodology, and correction policy or compare the complete skin-poll representation set.
