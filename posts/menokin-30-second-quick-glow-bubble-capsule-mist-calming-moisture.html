A rounded 0% felt-irritation share should never be read as zero risk.

Evidence boundary: A 5.0 average, 97% five-star share, and favorable shopper polls do not prove that this product hydrates, calms inflammation, creates glow, improves makeup wear, repairs a barrier, treats a condition, or is safe for every sensitive-skin user.
